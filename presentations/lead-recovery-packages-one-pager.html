Scope assumptions

Pricing assumes normal usage for one operating business location with typical lead-response and follow-up volume. Unusually high conversation volume, multi-location rollout, heavy custom workflow requests, paid media management, or unusually high third-party model/tool consumption may require a revised scope or additional fees.

The service is designed to stay simple and flat-rate for normal operating use while still leaving room to quote larger or more complex environments fairly.

Included scope assumptions

  • One primary business/entity unless otherwise quoted
  • Normal monthly usage based on expected service-business lead flow
  • Third-party hard costs, ad spend, premium integrations, and unusual overages excluded unless explicitly included
  • Custom builds outside the agreed workflow scope quoted separately

Recommended starting path

A simple four-step process to plug the leaks, launch the system, and start recovering revenue.

1. Confirm the leak

Identify where missed calls, web leads, estimates, or review follow-up are currently slipping through.

2. Select the package

Choose Starter, Growth, or Managed Growth based on lead volume, workflow needs, and reporting depth.

3. Configure the system

Set up routing, follow-up logic, messaging, and reporting around the business’s actual operating flow.

4. Launch and refine

Go live, monitor performance, and tighten the system based on real lead behavior and response gaps.
