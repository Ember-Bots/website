2-Minute Sales Video

Use EmberBots.ai as the story source, then sell relief from operational drag.

This presentation turns the live Ember Bots website positioning into a 2-minute sales video package. It is built for service-based SMBs, especially home-services owners, and focuses on slow follow-up, missed calls, scattered systems, and the practical automation systems Ember Bots deploys to fix them.

Audience
Service-based SMBs and home-services owners
Runtime
105–120 seconds
Source
Live EmberBots.ai positioning
Goal
Explain the offer and drive interest
Core message

What the video should sell

  • Ember Bots helps small businesses automate lead response, follow-up, scheduling, customer communication, onboarding, and back-office work
  • the value is reduced operational drag and tighter execution
  • the promise is practical: faster response, less manual work, better organization
Sales angle

The business is not selling “AI.” It is selling relief from the drag that slows revenue and operations.

The sales story should stay grounded in the live site language: slow follow-up, missed calls, scattered systems, repetitive admin, and systems built around how the business actually runs.

Pain

Missed calls, slow follow-up, manual scheduling, repeated data entry, and admin sprawl.

Shift

Ember Bots removes operational drag with automation systems configured to the business.

Outcome

Faster response, tighter operations, less repetitive work, and more owner clarity.

Voiceover script

2-minute narration structure

0:00–0:12

Small businesses do not usually lose because they lack hustle. They lose time, money, and momentum to slow follow-up, manual admin, scattered systems, and routine work that should not require owner attention.

0:12–0:25

A lead comes in and sits too long. A missed call turns into missed revenue. Scheduling happens by hand. Customer messages get scattered across channels. Teams re-enter the same information again and again.

0:25–0:38

That operational drag compounds quietly. It slows response, creates inconsistency, drains the team, and holds the business back even when demand is already there.

0:38–0:52

Ember Bots exists to remove that drag. We build automation systems for small businesses that help them respond faster, follow up more consistently, stay organized, and reduce repetitive operational work.

0:52–1:10

That includes lead response automation, customer message routing, scheduling and reminder automation, client onboarding automation, internal task orchestration, follow-up automation, back-office admin automation, and executive daily briefings.

1:10–1:24

Instead of forcing a business into generic software workflows, Ember Bots maps each system to the way the company already runs — its team, tools, business hours, escalation rules, and customer flow.

1:24–1:39

The result is practical. Leads get handled faster. Follow-up stops slipping through the cracks. Scheduling gets cleaner. Customer communication becomes easier to manage. Teams spend less time on repetitive admin and more time on conversations, service, and decisions.

1:39–1:52

Ember Bots works best for service-based SMBs with steady inbound leads, real customer communication volume, and repeatable operational bottlenecks that are stealing time from growth.

1:52–2:00

If your business is losing time to slow response, manual follow-up, and operational clutter, Ember Bots can help you fix it. Start at EmberBots.ai.
