Missed Revenue Snapshot
The diagnostic

Start with proof, not generic AI promises.

The snapshot gives the sales team a specific reason to call: Ember reviewed the public buying path and found places where good leads may be slowing down before they book.

What we look for

Unclear phone, booking, or contact path that creates friction for urgent buyers.
Web forms or estimate requests with no clear follow-up expectation.
No obvious after-hours capture or missed-call recovery path.
Review themes suggesting callback, scheduling, estimate, or communication friction.

Who fits first

Owner-led HVAC, plumbing, and home-service operators.
Strong local footprint with enough demand to make follow-up valuable.
Visible public evidence that the customer journey can be tightened.
Teams great at service delivery, but inconsistent in front-office execution.
Snapshot output

Company fit

Local proof, reputation signal, territory fit, and why the account deserves a human call.

Evidence

Lead-leak signals

Two to four specific observations pulled from public pages, reviews, forms, and contact paths.

Action

First fix

Missed-call text-back, web-lead response, estimate follow-up, after-hours capture, or review workflow.

Important standard: We do not invent missed calls, revenue numbers, or private operating details. The snapshot uses specific public evidence and careful language so the outreach stays credible.
