Sales Strategy
6-month GTM direction

Brand broad. Sell narrow.

Ember Bots should position broadly around revenue recovery for SMBs, then execute narrowly where pain is obvious, ROI is measurable, and sales proof can compound fast.

Core strategy

  • Lead with revenue recovery, not AI jargon
  • Win the first wedge before expanding
  • Use the Snapshot to open deals
  • Sell implementation and monthly optimization behind it

Ember Bots
Positioning

Sell the outcome, not the platform.

The right market story is simple: Ember Bots helps small businesses recover missed revenue by improving response speed, follow-up, and lead handling.

Do not lead with

  • generic AI automation language
  • custom bots as the product
  • platform abstractions
  • broad “we help every SMB” messaging
Cleaner positioning creates cleaner sales conversations

ICP

Best-fit buyers already have demand.

The win condition is not “find anyone who needs AI.” It is finding operators who already have inbound activity and are leaking revenue in the handoff.

  • owner-led SMB
  • local service operator
  • real website and reputation
  • visible inbound lead flow
  • inconsistent response or follow-up
