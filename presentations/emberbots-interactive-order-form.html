Agreement structure

This order form works together with the Ember Bots Master Services Agreement. If there is a conflict, the Order Form controls for the specific services ordered.

Activation and billing

Setup fees are due on signing. The first monthly payment is due at Activation, defined as when the first customer-facing or operational automation, chatbot, or AI agent is live and available for Customer use.

Initial term and cancellation

Monthly services have a three-month minimum initial term. After that, services renew month-to-month unless either party cancels with at least 30 days’ written notice.

Ownership

Customer owns its business data and content. Ember Bots retains ownership of its platform, templates, systems, reusable components, and internal methods. Subject to payment, Customer receives the right to use configured live deliverables for its internal business operations.

Service credits

Credits may apply only for qualifying Ember Bots-controlled outages of covered live services, and do not apply to planned maintenance, third-party outages, customer-caused issues, force majeure, or unpaid accounts.

AI limitations

AI agents and automations may produce inaccurate, incomplete, delayed, or unsuitable outputs. The services are not a substitute for legal, medical, financial, tax, or other regulated professional advice, and Ember Bots does not guarantee business results.

Limitation of liability

Ember Bots’ aggregate liability is limited as described in the governing agreement, and consequential or lost-profit damages are excluded to the fullest extent permitted by law.
