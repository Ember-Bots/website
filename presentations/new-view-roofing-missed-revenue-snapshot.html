Missed Revenue Snapshot

New View Roofing looks like a high-fit Ember Bots prospect.

New View already shows strong trust signals in the Dallas-Fort Worth market. The real opportunity is tightening the hidden revenue leaks around lead response, missed calls, inspection scheduling, estimate follow-up, and insurance coordination.

Why the fit is strong

  • Local, reputation-driven roofing operator
  • Urgent inbound demand from leaks and storm damage
  • Phone-heavy inspection and estimate workflow
  • High-ticket jobs where speed-to-lead matters
  • Insurance-related admin that creates real drag

Where revenue is probably leaking

Lead response
Their site promises follow-up within 24 hours. For roofing, especially urgent jobs, that may already be too slow.
Missed calls
After-hours or storm-spike calls can disappear fast if there is no automatic capture and immediate re-engagement.
Post-inspection drop-off
A strong inspection does not guarantee a closed job. Manual quote follow-up is a common leak point.

Operational friction signals

  • Manual scheduling back-and-forth
  • Insurance-claim communication burden
  • Limited visibility into stuck opportunities

Why this matters

  • Roofing is high-value and timing-sensitive
  • The first credible responder often wins
  • Small response gains can produce meaningful ROI

What Ember Bots could implement

Customer-facing systems

  • Instant lead acknowledgment
  • Missed-call text-back workflows
  • Inspection booking and reminders
  • Estimate follow-up sequences

Internal systems

  • Lead routing by service type
  • Task orchestration for handoffs
  • Daily pipeline summaries
  • Stalled-opportunity alerts

Best first offer

  • Missed Revenue Snapshot diagnostic
  • Lead-response and scheduling audit
  • Follow-up gap analysis

Expected impact

  • More inspections booked
  • Fewer leads lost after hours
  • Less admin drag on project managers

The positioning is simple.

New View Roofing already appears to have the credibility, service quality, and local trust. The question is whether lead response, scheduling, follow-up, and coordination are performing at the same level as the brand.

Bottom line
  • This is a strong-fit Ember Bots target.
  • Lead with revenue leakage, not generic AI.
